{{ icon("download", "ico ico-title") }}
  1. Open Secureddy in Safari.Other iPhone browsers cannot install it.
  2. Tap ShareThe square with an arrow, at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen", then Add.Secureddy opens like an app, full screen, and you stay signed in.
